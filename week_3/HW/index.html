Black Truffle Cream Pasta

A creamy and flavorful pasta with mushrooms and truffle oil. This recipe is simple to make and works well for a quick dinner.

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 150g mushrooms
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 2 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 teaspoon truffle oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Truffle salt (optional)

Instructions

  1. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta according to the package instructions.
  2. While the pasta is cooking, slice the mushrooms and mince the garlic.
  3. Melt the butter in a pan over medium heat.
  4. Add the mushrooms and cook until they become soft and lightly browned.
  5. Add the garlic and cook for about 30 seconds.
  6. Pour in the heavy cream and stir until the sauce begins to thicken.
  7. Add the Parmesan cheese and mix well.
  8. Add the cooked pasta to the sauce and toss until evenly coated.
  9. Turn off the heat and add the truffle oil.
  10. Season with salt, black pepper, and truffle salt if desired.

Tips

  • Do not cook truffle oil over high heat because it can lose its aroma.
  • Save a little pasta water before draining the pasta. Add it to the sauce if the sauce becomes too thick.
  • Baby bella mushrooms have a stronger flavor than white button mushrooms.
